ABOUT GLAMORA AR

Beauty without limits.

Glamora AR brings together beauty, creativity and augmented reality to create a completely new way to experience makeup.

OUR VISION

The future of beauty is interactive.

Glamora AR was created with a simple idea: beauty should be something you can experience, not just something you see on a shelf.

With virtual try-on technology, users can experiment with lipstick, eyeliner, blush and other beauty products before deciding what they love.

Our goal is to make discovering beauty easier, more creative and more personal.
